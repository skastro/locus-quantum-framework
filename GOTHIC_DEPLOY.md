## 🎯 FONT PAIRING BREAKDOWN

- Maximum medieval impact

- Sets the tone immediately

**Vibe:** 1600s grimoire, medieval scholar, ancient library

### **Cinzel - All Other Headings + UI**
```
Used for: Section titles, card titles, buttons, labels, metadata
Font: 'Cinzel', serif  
Weights: 400, 600, 700, 900
Letter-spacing: 0.05em - 0.4em (varies by use)
```

**Why Here:**
- Roman warrior monument
- Strong, dignified, readable
- Professional + mystical
- Works at all sizes

**Vibe:** Carved stone, imperial decree, timeless authority

### **Georgia - Body Text (Unchanged)**
```
Used for: Paragraphs, descriptions, quotes
Font: 'Georgia', serif
Weights: 400 (regular), italic
```

**Why Here:**
- Highly readable
- Classic serif warmth
- Perfect contrast with gothic headers
- Keeps accessibility high

## 🎨 DESIGN DECISIONS EXPLAINED

### **Why NOT IM Fell English Everywhere?**
- Too ornate for small sizes
- Harder to read in paragraphs
- Would overwhelm the content
- Best impact when used selectively

### **Why NOT Just Cinzel Everywhere?**
- Missing the dramatic medieval punch
- Artist name needs maximum impact
- IM Fell + Cinzel = perfect one-two punch
- Variety creates hierarchy

### **Why Keep Georgia for Body?**
- Don't fight readability
- Body text needs maximum clarity
- Georgia is warm, inviting, classic
- Perfect contrast with gothic headers

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

### **Font Loading:**
Both fonts are loaded from Google Fonts - free, fast, reliable:
```css
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IM+Fell+English&family=Cinzel:wght@400;600;700;900&display=swap');
```

### **Performance:**
- IM Fell English: Only 1 weight loaded (400)
- Cinzel: 4 weights loaded (400, 600, 700, 900)
- Total: 5 font files
- Still very lightweight

### **Mobile Optimization:**
- All fonts scale fluidly
- Larger touch targets
- Readable at all sizes
- Tested on iOS and Android

### **Fallbacks:**
```css
font-family: "IM Fell English", serif;
font-family: "Cinzel", serif;
font-family: "Georgia", serif;
```
If Google Fonts fail to load, all fall back to system serif fonts
